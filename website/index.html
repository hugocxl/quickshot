Capture your screen with both Command keys and paste it into your tools.

  • Version {{version}}
  • macOS 14 or later
  • Apple silicon and Intel

Quickshot is a small, native screenshot utility for Mac. It saves the display under your pointer as a full-resolution PNG and puts the file’s path on your clipboard. Show a coding agent what you see, share a UI bug with a terminal tool, or save a reference without breaking your flow.

  • Fig 0.1 {{vector keyboard-shortcuts}}

    Press both Command keys

    Left and right together, from any app, without switching windows.

  • Fig 0.2 {{vector image-replacement}}

    Capture the screen

    The display under your pointer, saved as a PNG at its native resolution.

  • Fig 0.3 {{vector cli-tooling}}

    Paste the path

    Into your terminal, a coding agent or any tool that accepts image paths.

Stay in flow

Capture from the menu bar or with both Command keys, and keep working where you are. A small preview confirms the shot without taking focus. Click it to open the PNG, or let it fade away.

Keep it or let it go

Save captures to your Desktop to keep them, or to temporary storage that cleans up after 24 hours. Changing the destination leaves earlier captures where they are.

Stays on your Mac

Screenshots never leave your Mac, and there is no account or upload service. Input Monitoring lets Quickshot recognize the two Command keys. It does not record your typing.

Made for macOS

A native Swift and AppKit app that lives in the menu bar, with no Dock icon and no third-party runtime dependencies. The preview respects Reduce Motion, and the shutter sound respects muted audio. The docs cover setup, storage and troubleshooting.
